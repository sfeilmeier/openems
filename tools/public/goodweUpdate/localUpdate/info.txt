This localUpdate tools where used to update a huge amouont of GoodWe 20 & 30 inverters in Natternberg semi-automatic.

Structure:
One Laptop
 - SSH connections via Kitty
 - Start goodweUpdateToolLokal.sh once (Guide that is not stopping itself)

5 FEMS devices
 - Preinstalled files:
   - /home/fems/arm.bin 
   - /home/fems/dsp.bin
   - /home/fems/goodweUpdateToolLokal.sh (Could be named different)
   - /home/fems/goodweUpdate.jar
 - Passwords as easy as possible for the electical installer, to establish connections in the mornign and mondays
